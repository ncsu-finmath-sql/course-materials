# Course Roadmap

Seven classes, one assignment after each. Every assignment can be completed offline with the practice
database, so a data-service outage never blocks you. Class demos may use WRDS.

| # | Assignment | Topics | Financial application | AI policy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Released** - Equity Price Exploration | `SELECT`, `WHERE`, `ORDER BY`, `LIMIT` | Equity price exploration | No AI |
| 2 | *Planned* | Aggregations, `GROUP BY` | Stock returns and trading volume | No AI |
| 3 | *Planned* | `JOIN`s | Equity prices and company fundamentals | No AI |
| 4 | *Planned* | CTEs, `CASE` | Foreign exchange analysis | **Copilot introduced** with clear boundaries |
| 5 | *Planned* | Dates and financial calculations | Bonds and yield data | Copilot optional |
| 6 | *Planned* | Window functions | Rolling returns and volatility | Copilot allowed |
| 7 | *Planned* | Integrated analysis | Multi-asset dataset | Copilot allowed |

The topic order is tentative and will be confirmed against the [syllabus](FIM590_Syllabus.pdf). Starter files for a later
assignment appear in your repository when it is released.

## Copilot progression

1. **Assignments 1-3:** you write all SQL yourself. Turn Copilot off (details in [copilot-policy.md](copilot-policy.md)).
2. **Assignment 4:** Copilot is introduced in class. You learn to critique what it writes.
3. **Assignment 5:** optional. **Assignments 6-7:** allowed.

Whenever AI is allowed, you are responsible for understanding, validating, and being able to explain every query you submit.
No grade ever depends on having Copilot access.
