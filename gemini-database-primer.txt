DATABASE INFORMATION
Engine: DuckDB. Tables: prices, companies, fundamentals, fx_rates, fx_pairs, fx_exposure.
The data is synthetic. Tickers and company names are fictional.

TABLE fundamentals
One row per company per fiscal quarter. Money amounts are in millions of US dollars. Share counts are in millions.
Rows: 60
Primary key: ticker, fiscal_year, fiscal_quarter

  fiscal_year INTEGER: fiscal year
  fiscal_quarter INTEGER: 1 to 4
  period_end_date DATE: last day of the quarter
  report_date DATE: date the results were published
  revenue DECIMAL(12,1): sales for the quarter, $ millions
  net_income DECIMAL(12,1): profit for the quarter, $ millions
  eps DECIMAL(8,2): earnings per share for the quarter, $
  shares_outstanding DECIMAL(10,1): shares outstanding, millions
  total_assets DECIMAL(12,1): at quarter end, $ millions
  total_debt DECIMAL(12,1): at quarter end, $ millions

TABLE fx_exposure
One row per company per foreign currency.
Rows: 19
Primary key: ticker, ccy
Columns:
  ticker VARCHAR: trading symbol (matches companies.ticker)
  ccy VARCHAR: three-letter currency code
  pct_revenue DECIMAL(5,2): share of the company's revenue earned in that currency, in percent (22.00 means 22%)
NULLs: none in any column
Distinct ccy: AUD, CAD, CHF, EUR, GBP, JPY
Sample rows:

  ALPH | EUR | 22.00
  ALPH | JPY | 9.00
  BRVO | CAD | 14.00
